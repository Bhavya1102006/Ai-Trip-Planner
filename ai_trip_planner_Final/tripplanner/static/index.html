The Science

How It Works

01

Parse your brief

A rule-based parser (or LLM) extracts destination, days, budget, interests and pace from your text.

02

Score & Select POIs

Every place of interest is scored against your interests, rating, and local-gem bonus, then the best pool is selected.

03

Cluster by Geography

K-means groups nearby stops into day-clusters, then a rebalancer ensures each day has roughly the same load.

04

Sequence each day

A greedy heuristic schedules each stop respecting opening hours, travel time, budget, and day-end cutoff.

About

Built with Purpose

TripAI is a Stage 2 prototype of an AI-powered itinerary planner. The engine uses geographic clustering, time-window scheduling, and interest-based scoring to produce realistic day-plans — no hallucinations, no impossible routes.

The planner actively promotes sustainable tourism: local, non-mainstream places receive a scoring bonus, nudging travellers away from overcrowded spots toward hidden gems that benefit local communities (aligned with UN SDG 8 & 11).
